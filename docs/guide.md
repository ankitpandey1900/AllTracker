# Using AllTracker

AllTracker is my place for planning study work, tracking what I actually did, and seeing whether I am keeping up with the plan.

## Sign in and sync

Use Google or GitHub to sign in. Once signed in, your tracker, tasks, routines, bookmarks, settings, phases, and timer state are stored against your account.

The app loads the browser cache first so it opens quickly, then checks the cloud in the background. If you use another laptop or phone, sign in with the same account and give the sync a moment to finish before editing lots of data.

## Tasks and routines

Tasks can be daily or weekly and have a priority. Completed tasks stay in history until you delete them yourself. Routines are separate time-based reminders for repeated work.

## Focus timer

Start a timer with the subject/category you are working on. The session is saved when it ends and is reflected in the dashboard, timeline, and profile statistics. If a session crosses midnight, the time is split between the two dates.

## Phases and tracker columns

Use Settings to create a phase and choose the columns/subjects used during that date range. Phases are saved individually, so changing a normal setting should not remove existing phases.

## Maamu and reminders

Maamu is meant to be direct. It can be supportive, tactical, or give a light roast when your work has been missing. Enable browser notifications from Settings if you want reminder slots, routine warnings, and public-study activity prompts.

The reminders work while AllTracker is open in the browser or installed PWA. They are not background push notifications yet, so a fully closed browser cannot receive them.

## Feed and privacy

The feed is for posts, comments, mentions, and replies. Your public profile and focus visibility settings control what other people can see on the leaderboard. Do not share personal details in posts.
