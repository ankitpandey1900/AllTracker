Questions.

Anything else is in the handbook, or open an issue on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. No paywall, no locked features, no ads. The timer, the log, the roadmap importer, the World Stage and Maamu are all included.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start tracking straight away and everything is saved in your browser. Sign in with Google or GitHub when you want the same data on your phone and laptop.

Does it work on my phone, or offline?

Both. AllTracker installs as an app from the browser. Your timer and log are kept on the device and sync to the cloud whenever you're signed in and online.

How does a streak count?

A day counts when it has at least two hours of logged study, or when you use a rest-day freeze (four per 30 days). Yesterday stays editable until 4 AM, and a session that crosses midnight is split between both days.

Who can see my data?

Your notes, tasks, bookmarks, roadmap and Maamu conversations are private to your account. Whether your profile and current subject show on the World Stage is your choice in settings.
