Yes, AllTracker is 100% free with no hidden charges, paywalls, or annoying ads. You get full access to every feature — including the study timer, syllabus roadmap, drawing whiteboard, and your study history — right from your very first day.
Yes! You can study and run the timer even when you don't have internet. Everything you do is saved safely on your device immediately. Whenever you go back online, all your study hours and progress update automatically.
Select the subject you want to work on and tap Start. Whenever you need to rest, stretch, or get water, tap Break to pause without losing your streak. When you finish, tap Stop. Your study time and break minutes are neatly organized into your daily and weekly progress summaries.
Maamu is your honest, no-excuses study coach. Instead of giving fake compliments or polite talk, Maamu checks how much you actually studied. If you skip study sessions or fall behind your targets, Maamu gives you straight, practical guidance to snap you back into action.
World Stage is a shared study board where you can see other students putting in genuine effort alongside you each day. As you log focused study hours and show up consistently, you earn points, level up, and stay inspired together.
Yes! You can add your full course or exam syllabus. Divide it into subjects and chapters, set target exam dates, and tick off topics as you finish them. The app shows you your completion percentage and estimates when you will complete everything.
Yes! You can use AllTracker on any phone, tablet, or laptop. Just open it in your browser and tap "Add to Home Screen". It opens in full screen just like a regular mobile app, loads instantly, and works smoothly wherever you are.
Yes, 100%. Your study notes, daily task checklists, and personal schedules stay completely private to you. If you decide to join the optional community board, only your public study hours and chosen username will ever be visible.
